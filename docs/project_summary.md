# RetailPulse AI - Project Summary

## Project Overview

RetailPulse AI is an end-to-end Data Science and Analytics project developed to help retail businesses make data-driven decisions using Machine Learning, Forecasting, Customer Analytics, and MLOps techniques.

The platform provides demand forecasting, customer segmentation, churn prediction, inventory optimization, model monitoring, and automated retraining capabilities through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

---

## Business Objectives

* Predict future product demand
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Identify customers at risk of churn
* Optimize inventory planning
* Monitor model performance
* Support data-driven business decisions

## Business Impact

RetailPulse AI helps organizations improve forecasting accuracy, reduce stockouts, identify valuable customers, increase retention, and monitor machine learning systems in production environments.
